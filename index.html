Why a club for doing nothing?

When something else can answer, draft and decide, the rare skill is being able to stop. No-mind practice is old, simple and free: sit, let thoughts pass like weather, come back to the breath.

  • Silence is the practice

    No gurus, no jargon, no streak to protect. Just a few quiet minutes and a place to come back to.

  • Warm, never preachy

    No belief required. Start where you are, in any chair, on any day.

  • Made by one real person

    A small, human-run club. We are still building it, and you can watch it grow.
